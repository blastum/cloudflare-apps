Revocable living trust

The grantor's main during-life vehicle: holds titled assets, names successor trustee for incapacity, and at death often splits into sub-trusts (bypass, QTIP, children's trusts) per a formula in the instrument. Does not by itself reduce estate tax.

Configure this vessel in the explorer

A will substitute you control during life. At death it is a funnel: it can pour into bypass, QTIP, pot, SNT, or dynasty shares. The RLT itself is not those trusts.

Probate avoidance and lifetime control; tax and protective jobs happen in the subtrusts it creates.

Do not mix

ClauseWhy
Spendthrift clauseYour creditors can generally reach a revocable trust — spendthrift-for-yourself is theater.
GST exemption allocationA revocable trust uses no GST exemption and builds no skip shield.
Dynasty / perpetualDynasty duration on a revocable trust is empty — the core is still yours.
Crummey withdrawal rightsGifts to yourself are not completed gifts.
