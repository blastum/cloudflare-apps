Separate share trust

Individual sub-trust or segregated account per beneficiary — equal or formula-fixed share from funding or immediately after a pot division. Trustee cannot reallocate between shares. For inter vivos lifetime funding with Crummey powers and per-beneficiary accounts, see Crummey trust (same structure, different timing and tax mechanics).
