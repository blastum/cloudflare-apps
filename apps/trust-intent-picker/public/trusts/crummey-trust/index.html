Crummey trust

Irrevocable inter vivos trust using temporary beneficiary withdrawal powers so lifetime contributions qualify for the annual gift exclusion. Drafted with separate-share sub-trusts or ledgered accounts per beneficiary so each powerholder's Crummey notice and withdrawal attach to their own share. This is the inter vivos form of separate-share planning — distinct from testamentary separate share trust (two-pot at death) and from ILIT (insurance premiums).

Irrevocable gift trust with notice and a temporary withdrawal right so each year’s transfer qualifies for the annual exclusion.

Move wealth during life using the annual exclusion, usually into separate shares.

Locked core

ClauseWhy
Irrevocable once fundedCompleted gift — you cannot take the money back.
Crummey withdrawal rightsWithout the right and notice, the gift is a future interest.
Separate shareAnnual exclusion is per donee; a shared pool fights that math.

What you can add

ClauseWhy
Spendthrift clauseOnce the withdrawal window lapses, lock the share against creditors.
HEMS / ascertainable standardHow tightly the trustee must justify later distributions.
Incentive clausesTie later payouts to school, work, or similar milestones.
Trustee removal / replacementSomeone can replace a bad trustee without giving the child the fund.
In terrorem / no-contestDiscourages a beneficiary from suing to break the gift.
GST exemption allocationIf grandchildren are donees, GST exemption is a separate knob from the annual exclusion.
Decanting provisionLets later trustees adapt terms without a court rewrite.

Do not mix

ClauseWhy
Pot / spray / sprinkleA spray pot is a hard fit with per-donee annual exclusion.
QTIP / marital deductionMarital deduction is a death-time estate job, not an annual-gift vehicle.
Special / supplemental needsA right to withdraw is a countable resource for a disabled donee — use an SNT share instead.
