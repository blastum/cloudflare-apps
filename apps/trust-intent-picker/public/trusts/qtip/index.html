QTIP trust

Qualified terminable interest property trust: surviving spouse must receive all trust income for life; no one else may receive principal during spouse's life. Executor elects QTIP treatment on Form 706. Gives marital deduction at first death while first decedent names remainder beneficiaries.

Defers estate tax via the marital deduction — spouse supported for life, remainder to children; assets stay in the survivor’s estate unless a later bypass or portability applies.

Qualify the rest of the estate for the marital deduction so tax is deferred, not eliminated.

Locked core

ClauseWhy
QTIP / marital deductionSpouse income for life plus the Form 706 QTIP election — that is the identity.
Spouse has no general power of appointmentNo general power in the spouse that would blow the QTIP rules.

ClauseWhy
