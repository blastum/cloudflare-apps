B trust (bypass / credit shelter)

At the first spouse's death, a formula funds this irrevocable sub-trust up to the available estate tax exclusion. Assets and their growth are meant to bypass the survivor's taxable estate at the second death. The survivor often receives income and HEMS principal access but does not own the trust outright.

Do not mix

ClauseWhy
QTIP / marital deductionIf it QTIPs, it is in the survivor’s estate — that is a different vessel sitting next to this one.
Crummey withdrawal rightsBypass is funded at death from the estate, not by annual-exclusion gifts.
Pure discretionary distributionsWide-open principal for the spouse can look like a general power and pull the fund back into their estate.
Special / supplemental needsA disabled remainder beneficiary needs an SNT share, not SNT rules for the spouse’s lifetime interest.
