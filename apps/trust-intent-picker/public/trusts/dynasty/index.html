Dynasty / GST-exempt trust

Long-duration irrevocable trust with GST exemption allocated (Form 709 or 706 Schedule R). Designed to benefit multiple generations without repeated federal estate/GST tax at each generation. Illinois limits duration (prudent-period rule, not true perpetuity).

Locks GST exemption into an irrevocable, long-duration trust so descendants can benefit for as many generations as Illinois (or the chosen situs) allows.

Multi-generation family wealth, not a one-time inheritance.

Locked core

ClauseWhy
Irrevocable once fundedExemption is used at funding; you cannot unwind it.
GST exemption allocationMarks the trust GST-exempt so later skips are not taxed again.
Dynasty / perpetualKeeps the trust alive for descendants within RAP / Illinois limits.
Descendants as a growing classFuture grandchildren and great-grandchildren are included.

What you can add

ClauseWhy

Pure discretionary distributionsWider trustee latitude — common on long-duration trusts.
Power of appointmentLimited power so a child can redirect among descendants, not out of the bloodline.
Decanting provisionLets later trustees adapt terms.
Incentive clausesIncentive language on a given descendant’s access.
Trustee removal / replacementTrust protector / removal so the trust can outlive the original trustee.
In terrorem / no-contestNo-contest, if you expect a fight.

Do not mix

ClauseWhy
QTIP / marital deductionMarital deduction puts assets in the survivor’s estate; dynasty wants them out.
Special / supplemental needsOne disabled descendant needs an SNT share — not SNT rules for the whole class.
Bypass / exemption funding formulaBypass is a first-death exemption shelter for the spouse. Dynasty is a multi-gen skip vehicle. They can be sequential, not the same clause set.
